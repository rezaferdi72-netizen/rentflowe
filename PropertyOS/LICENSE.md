# Ownership and third-party components

The custom PropertyOS source code and project files produced for this project
are delivered to the requesting application owner, who may use, modify, build,
operate, redistribute and maintain them independently. No vendor-controlled
activation or license check is included.

Third-party open-source libraries, bundled fonts and their transitive dependencies
retain their respective licenses and copyright notices. Ownership of PropertyOS
does not transfer ownership of those upstream projects. Keep their license notices
when redistributing a build. Bundled font notices are included in `public/`.

This first development release is provided without a claim of production
certification or a guarantee of fitness. Review the feature status and validate
native targets before operating a live production portfolio.
